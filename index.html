Why Apistogrammarama?

Dwarf cichlids demand precision. Water chemistry, temperature, feeding, breeding — get it wrong and you lose the fish. Get it right, and you unlock one of aquascaping's most rewarding challenges.

Apistogrammarama is built by aquarists for aquarists. Deep-dive species profiles, care fundamentals backed by keeper experience, breeding guides for the next generation, and the history of how the hobby was built.
