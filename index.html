Small fish, exacting standards

Dwarf cichlids demand precision. Water chemistry, temperature, feeding, breeding — get it wrong and you lose the fish. Get it right, and you unlock one of the aquarium hobby's most rewarding challenges: fish that court, fight, and raise young right against the glass.

Apistogrammarama is built by aquarists for aquarists. Species profiles that go past the pet-store label, care fundamentals backed by keeper experience, breeding guides for the next generation, and the regional variants that make this genus worth chasing for decades.
